cap-evolve, from the idea to the threads

An onboarding course for a software engineer who joins the cap-evolve team. It starts with the motivation and goes deep into the code: the object model, the execution and threading model, the gate math, and the tests. The slides are in English. Every slide has speaker notes in English and in Hebrew.

How to run the course

One screen (notes under the slide)

  1. Pick the notes language above.
  2. Click Present with notes on a chapter.
  3. The notes panel opens under the slide. N hides or shows it.
  4. Move with → / Space and ←. Some slides show their parts one key press at a time.

Two screens (projector + presenter view)

  1. Click Slides only and move that window to the projector. Press F for full screen.
  2. Press P in it. A presenter window opens with the current slide, the next slide, the notes and a timer.
  3. Keep the presenter window on your laptop screen. Moving in either window moves both.
  4. If the browser blocks the pop-up, allow pop-ups for this page and press P again.

Reading copy and PDF

  • Handout shows every slide with its notes, one after another.
  • Print it to PDF (⌘ P → Save as PDF) for a copy to read or send ahead.
  • Inside any deck, D switches the handout on and off.

Timing

  • Each slide's notes start with a time marker, for example [4:00–5:30].
  • Every chapter adds up to about 20 minutes.
  • The presenter window shows a timer and the target length. Restart timer resets it.
