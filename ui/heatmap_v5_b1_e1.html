Read the sd column, not just the mean. 68% of these tasks return a different reward across identical repeated trials (mean trial sd 0.145). A single task's before/after difference below roughly 0.25 is indistinguishable from noise at 5 trials; the suite mean is resolved to ~0.02. MEAN rows pool the tasks named in them and are a convenience, not a claim that the groups are commensurable.
