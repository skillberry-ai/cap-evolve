Methodology differences: we start from task's OWN shipped skills; EvoSkill bootstraps from Anthropic's `skill-creator` meta-skill given only the task instruction. We do 4 iterations; they do 5 evolution × ~15 surrogate = up to 75 inner cycles. They have a surrogate verifier (worth +30 pp per their ablation); we don't. Their agent is Opus 4.6; ours is Sonnet 5. On the same 43-task subset, performance is roughly comparable within 5 pp — the 87-task gap comes from our infra-blocked tasks.
