Research questions

RQ1

How much of reported capability-optimization gain survives scorer correction and fresh held-out tasks?

Re-score history under both scorers (done, zero LLM cost). E03 compliance diagnostic. Re-measure seed and champions on a fresh final set. Experiments: E00, E03, E10.

RQ2

Under matched budgets, how do hill-climb, agent-optimize and official competitors compare?

Same surface, models, feedback, evaluator and budget (matched track) plus native designs at equal total budget (practical track). Controls: seed, compliance instruction, one-shot repair, best-of-K. Experiment: E10.

RQ3

Which mechanisms explain any gain: gate, footprint masking, DAG and merge, active evaluation, surface width?

Development-data ablations at equal budgets. Experiments: E11 to E16.

RQ4

Do frozen artifacts transfer across models and benchmarks?

No re-optimization. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, GPT-5.5, SpreadsheetBench 2 if available. Smaller-optimized vs larger-unoptimized at measured deployment cost. Experiments: E20 to E22.

RQ5

Do the measurement controls prevent false improvements: null edits, injected faults, gold isolation?

Null-edit candidates through each gate, injected fault cases, gold-isolation negative test, footprint false-exclusion audit. Experiments: E04 to E07.

Paper route. Chosen after the evidence: systems (A), method (B) or empirical study (C). The novelty screen leaves only narrow claims (S1 to S6 in literature/novelty_matrix.md section 3). Route B needs S1 or S2 to show a gain under matched budget. Otherwise route C is built on S3 to S5. S5 (hill-climb vs agent-optimize at matched total budget) does not require a win: a simpler method winning is reported as such.

Rules

  • Routing: a model id starting with rits/ goes to the RITS deployment with the RITS key header; everything else goes to the LiteLLM gateway with a bearer key. Credentials are never logged.
  • Metering tag: the bearer token or x-api-key is read; if it starts with tag: it is the call's tag, format tag:<exp>:<arm>:<role>, otherwise untagged.
  • Budget guard: once capped_tokens reaches max_nonrits_tokens_ex_cache_read (100,000,000) every call gets HTTP 429 naming the cap. Counting uses total_tokens when usage has it (OpenAI shape), else input + output + cache-creation (Anthropic shape), so cache reads are excluded. RITS tokens are added to rits_tokens and never capped. The ledger is rebuilt from the call log on restart.
  • Retries and timeouts: the gateway does no retries; upstream timeout 900 s; a transport exception returns 502.
  • Log fields (logs/gateway_calls.jsonl): t (UTC, written at call end), tag, model, upstream, api, stream, status, queue_s, latency_s, usage (raw upstream object), error (first 300 chars).
  • Live health: capped (non-RITS) tokens 1,728,497 of 100,000,000; RITS tokens 70,870,100; 14,896 calls; blocked = False.

Final campaign

  • Scored once. All frozen artifacts and seeds are scored once, interleaved in randomized order, in one short window. This controls provider drift. Model snapshot ids and timestamps are recorded per rollout.
  • Trials. Suite A: 2 per task. Suite B: 4 per task. Every confirmation and final trial uses new seeds. Whether the providers honour seeds is verified first.

  • Reporting. All registered arms and seeds are reported, whether or not they look good. A crashed or aborted search stays in the denominator and its champion is the seed artifact unless a candidate had already been promoted.

Rules

  • Unit of inference: the task, clustered by family; trials averaged only within a task.
  • Seeds are a separate level. S=3 gives 2 degrees of freedom, so pipeline intervals use t(2) = 4.30. Seed-level percentile bootstraps are not the headline for S below 8.
  • Intervals: below 30 families the headline CI is the cluster-robust t interval (CR1), not the percentile bootstrap. Sign-flip randomization is a declared robustness check.
  • Multiplicity: Holm step-down at FWER 0.05 within a suite. Each suite is its own family. A cross-suite claim needs both to pass. Unlisted contrasts are exploratory.
  • "Win" language only if the Holm-adjusted p is below 0.05 and the CI excludes 0 under the pessimistic missingness bound. Otherwise "no detectable difference at the MDE of X".
  • Claims are artifact-level (about the S champions) unless S is at least 5; pipeline-level claims need S of at least 5.
  • Never reported: SplitResult.stderr / combined_stderr (double counts within-task noise; overstates new-task variance by 12 to 33% at the assumed kappa and fixed-task variance by 1.2 to 8.6x).
  • SD labels its unit; SE names its sampling process; every table cell states n tasks, n families, n seeds, n trials per task.

Missingness

  • Failures are classified by a frozen deterministic classifier, blind to arm and reward. Unclassified counts as failure.
  • Exogenous failures (429, 5xx, gateway timeout, container pull, host OOM): up to 3 further attempts, same policy on every arm, triggered by error class only, cost charged to the arm. Exhausted retries make a missing cell.
  • Artifact-induced failures count as a trial with reward 0. They are never dropped.
  • Complete-case analysis only if the missing fraction is at most 5% per arm and missing counts are not associated with arm (Fisher exact p at least 0.10). Otherwise bounds only.
  • Pessimistic and optimistic bounds always sit next to the estimate. A contrast is "robust" only if the pessimistic-bound CI excludes 0.
  • Confirmatory evaluation needs at least 95% valid (arm, task) cells after retries, else the suite is re-executed or declared invalid for the headline. Coverage is computed on the paired intersection.

Cost accounting

Source: analysis_plan.md section 9. Money is metered from provider usage logs.

  • Per role: optimizer, target, simulator, judge, diagnostics. Optimizer-model cost and deployed-target cost are separate columns. A combined column appears only when both are fully priced.
  • Optimization cost C_opt(method, seed): proposer plus target rollouts on dev data (screening and confirmation included) plus judges and diagnostics. Tokens, USD, rollouts and wall hours, mean and SD over seeds. Failed and aborted searches are included.
  • Cost per usable champion for "run S searches, deploy the best on dev" is S x mean C_opt. Its accuracy has a winner's-curse bias, so it gets its own final-set row.
  • Deployment cost: metered target cost of one deployed task attempt on the final tasks, including failures and retries; also cost per success.
  • Net value per task v(a) = R x p_a - c_a, with the task reward R declared in advance. R=0 gives the cost-only view.
  • Break-even volume V* = C_opt / (v(champion) - v(baseline)) tasks. Defined only when the lower CI bound of the value difference is above 0; otherwise "no break-even established" with the denominator's interval. Never a delta-method interval when the denominator CI includes 0.
  • Unknown prices: RITS targets and GPT-5.5 are "unknown". Sums that include them are flagged "lower bound". Token counts and wall time are always reported. Optimizer cost is shown at proxy price, labelled as proxy.

Optimizer spend: bound and measured. One search at the 4M-token ceiling costs between $8 (all input at $2/MTok) and $40 (all output at $10/MTok). The pilot mix is far from either end: pilot_hc spent about $0.54 for 136k non-cache-read tokens (cache writes and reads dominate), about $3.95 per MTok. At that mix a ceiling search is about $16 and a 10-iteration Claude-Code hill-climb about $2.7. Non-RITS spend so far is about $2.2 proxy across E10 and E10B. The $1000 cap does not bind at the forecast; the 100M-token cap binds only if most searches reach the ceiling. Competitor optimizer spend per search is not recorded yet.
