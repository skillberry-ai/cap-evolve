\section{Limitations and Discussion}
\label{sec:v2:limits}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
\item \emph{We built the system and ran the baselines.} Tuning effort is asymmetric. Competitors run through adapters, WikiSkill is a community port, and every deviation is listed with its expected direction of bias (Appendix~\ref{app:config}). On Suites B and C the \capevolve{} arms edit prompt and tools while GEPA edits the prompt only; Suite A and the prompt-only cells of Suite D are the tie-breakers.
\item \emph{Power.} Three search seeds give two degrees of freedom for statements about the method, and the artifact-level minimum detectable effect is \mdeBCondThree{} points on Suite B and \mdeCCondThree{} on Suite C. We claim at the artifact level and call smaller gaps inconclusive; if no contrast clears Holm, the contribution is the protocol and the audit.
\item \emph{Exposure.} The Suite A final set samples an older release whose ids were scored at most twice before, score only. The Suite B and D test tasks are scored once and not used by search, but their task files were visible on the runner filesystem; a scan of 68 optimizer transcripts and the run directories found no access, which is absence of evidence. Suite C's test is development-exposed. Pretraining contamination cannot be checked.
\item \emph{Gates.} Rule P is conservative and, in the search-time data, held the seed artifact in most searches; the final evaluation decides whether that is reliability or inertia. Stock \capevolve{} gates on a split that also feeds the proposer, so a candidate can overfit it; we report selection-to-final shrinkage. The stock-gate arm on Suite B was added after the search-time results, so its rows are post-search-time, and agent-optimize on Suites B and C could read its own optimization and selection gold, so its rows carry a dagger and a sensitivity row without it.
\item \emph{Matched checkpoint.} Capacity bound the search, so $N^\star$ falls below the nominal budget and results say little about larger budgets. We match target rollouts, not optimizer compute.
\item \emph{Ablations.} Suite D uses another target and judge than Suites A to C and establishes mechanism, not rank; surface claims rest on it alone. The descriptions-only emulation of the MCP surface and the skill-package cell were not run, so those surfaces are not evaluated, and intake is tested by fault injection, not a user study.
\item \emph{Scorer, judge and metering.} LibreOffice fails 19 of 400 official gold files as outputs. GPT-5.5 judges the $\tau^2$ assertions on Suites B and C. Tokens of the internal service and of Azure have no visible price, and the gateway ledger over-counts Suite B by 20 to 60\% against transcripts.
\end{itemize}
