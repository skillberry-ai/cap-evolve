\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A reported gain from an agent optimizer is a measurement before it is a capability. In our audit, scorer compliance, split reuse and gold access explained much of an earlier gain, and the protocol we built removes each. Under it, \capevolve{} and nine competing methods are compared at one rollout checkpoint on final tasks that search never used. \needs{Two sentences on the headline outcome per suite, including whether a control or simpler method is competitive, after the final evaluation.} Whichever arm leads, the isolation tests, the rule-P operating characteristics and the matched-checkpoint procedure let others check a gain before they believe it.
