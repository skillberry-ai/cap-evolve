\subsection{Rule P trades power for fewer false promotions}
\label{sec:results:p}

In simulation with ten candidates and the real task counts, rule P (Section~\ref{sec:method:P}) promotes a null candidate in \simPFalseA{} of searches on Suite A, against \simKoneFalseA{} for the one-stage gate.

\subsection{Infrastructure incidents}
\label{sec:results:ops}

A stale gateway priority tag kept EvoSkill rollouts queued from 13:05 to 18:40 UTC on the first day, so its three seeds restarted from scratch.
Gateway 502 errors hit 1 to 2.5\% of Gemma calls until a retry went in at 13:11, and until then a failed rollout scored zero for competitor arms but was dropped for CapEvolve.
On Suite B, the agent-optimize arm can read gold actions for its 74 optimization and selection tasks, never for the sealed test set.
Appendix~\ref{app:incidents} lists every incident with its effect on the data.
