\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:v2:abl}

Each ablation changes one factor against the same reference cell and states the claim it tests, with a verdict in the pre-specified vocabulary: supported, equivalent, inconclusive or contradicted (cells and contrasts in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, Appendix~\ref{app:v2results}).

\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/fig_ablate.pdf}
\caption{A design choice matters only if its interval excludes the reference's. Each panel varies one factor against its reference on the same target, as the difference to the seed in points with 95\% intervals (placeholder). Table~\ref{tab:ablation} lists the cells and the registered contrasts.}
\label{fig:ablate}
\end{figure*}

Surface width. Suite D holds the loop, the stock gate, the budget and the optimizer fixed and edits the prompt only, tools only, or both. The claim is that prompt and tools beats prompt only on tasks whose failures need deterministic enforcement. \needs{Suite D surface grid: paired gains of P, T and P+T over the seed, P+T against each single surface, the descriptive prompt-tools interaction with its interval, both intervals, and the verdict. Do not write ``surface width matters'' unless the contrast exceeds the 10-point margin.}

Orchestration. The two shipped configurations differ in controller, parent selection, screen design, accept rule, turn limit (80 against 300) and, on Suite B, gold visibility, so the comparison is of bundles. The claim is that the three frozen hill-climb and the three frozen agent-optimize artifacts are equivalent within 10 points on Suites A and B, with seeds that return the seed artifact reported separately and excluded from equivalence statements. In headless mode the agent-optimize agent can end its turn while an evaluation it opened is still pending, and it cannot resume; on Suite A one seed stopped itself after 48 turns with its first candidate abandoned and the seed selected, having booked none of its 50 rounds, and the Suites B and C seeds ended their searches early as well. This is a property of agent-optimize as shipped, and the work it wastes is real. \needs{Final-set equivalence verdict (TOST, with its power) per suite, and the count of agent-optimize searches that ended early, from the final evaluation.}

Replication. Suite E repeats the Suite D cells on $\tau^2$ airline and only corroborates Suite D. Table~\ref{tab:suitee} (Appendix~\ref{app:v2results}) lists each Suite E contrast beside its Suite D counterpart. \needs{D-versus-E replication: for each Suite D contrast, whether the Suite E contrast has the same sign and an artifact-level interval that excludes zero (replicated) or not. No equivalence claim on Suite E.}

Gate. Rule P is a conservative configuration, and the stock gate is the shipped default. Table~\ref{tab:e12} (Appendix~\ref{app:v2results}) compares them at the same rollout count on Suites A and C with promotion counts, and Suite D adds a one-stage gate. The simulated and empirical operating characteristics are in Section~\ref{sec:v2:evaluation}: on repeated evaluations of an unmodified seed rule P promotes a null candidate in \nullRulePTen{}\% of ten-candidate searches against \nullOneStageTen{}\% for the one-stage rule. In the search-time data (post-search-time, each method's own gating split, not held-out), at the 12 October snapshot, with some searches still running, rule P hill-climb held the seed artifact as incumbent in \stInertHcA{} of \stSearchesHcA{} searches on Suite A, \stInertHcB{} of \stSearchesHcB{} on B and \stInertHcC{} of \stSearchesHcC{} on C, and agent-optimize in \stInertAoA{} of \stSearchesAoA{}, \stInertAoB{} of \stSearchesAoB{} and \stInertAoC{} of \stSearchesAoC{}. The stock gate promoted \stPromoStockA{} candidates per search on Suite A and \stPromoStockC{} on Suite C at that snapshot (Table~\ref{tab:attrib-promotions}, Table~\ref{tab:searchtime-gate}). Whether the strictness buys held-out reliability or only inertia is answered by the final evaluation, and the verdict stays open until then. \needs{Final-set gain, promotions per search, inert searches and selection-to-final shrinkage for stock gate, rule P and one-stage gate; null-edit false-accept rates from the empirical null pool.}

History and intake. The history cell (E26 on Suite D, E36 on Suite E) makes history unavailable: it is neither staged, nor reachable in the run directory, nor inherited through the parent's snapshot (Appendix~\ref{app:history}). It compares with the reference cell; the claim is that removing them raises the number of previously passing tasks that regress and lowers the final gain. \needs{Suite D history-off result: regressed tasks, accepted candidates, final gain, verdict.} The descriptions-only and skill-package cells were not run (Section~\ref{sec:v2:limits}). The intake gate was tested by injecting scorer and adapter faults. \needs{Fault-injection summary (pass, partial, gap) with the gaps named.}

\subsection{What each surface is worth}
\label{sec:v2:types}

Marginal value comes from the Suite D grid: each surface's gain over the seed, and the interaction of prompt and tools. \needs{Suite D marginal values with intervals.} The failure types that the seed fails on come from deterministic labels on search-time data, not from the final set: on the $\tau^2$ suites most failed seed tasks are a forgotten required step or a tool call with wrong arguments (Table~\ref{tab:attrib-failure-types}; the surface-by-failure table is Table~\ref{tab:attrib-surface-failure}; both in Appendix~\ref{app:v2results}), failures that tool code could in principle enforce, while on Suite A they are output-contract and knowledge failures. Which surface fixed which failure type is an attribution over search-time candidates, and E10 cannot separate surfaces because the main arms edit them together, so we report attribution only as hypothesis-generating description and draw the decision guide from Suite D cells alone. Suite D uses gpt-4.1-mini, so transfer to gpt-oss and Gemma is untested. Table~\ref{tab:decomp} splits Suite D champions that edit both surfaces into the effect of their prompt edits and of their tool edits. \needs{Decision guide: a numbered rule list, each rule traceable to a Suite D or attribution table cell.} The MCP tool surface and the skill package are not evaluated.
