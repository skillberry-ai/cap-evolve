# R5: Writing editor review of "CapEvolve: Optimizing Any Editable Surface of a Frozen Agent Under Code-Owned Evaluation"

Scope: live body files (paper.tex includes sections/v2_*.tex and related.tex; the older intro.tex, method.tex, setup.tex, etc. are not compiled and are ignored), plus appendix and table captions. Placeholders (\pend, \needs, mock figures) ignored for values. All paths below are under `/home/dev/projects2/campaign-scratch/reviews/draft4/research/capevolve-paper/paper/`. Guide applied: writing-guide.md at story, section, paragraph, sentence, word and graphics level.

## 1. Does the story come through?

Story as a reader would reconstruct it. What: CapEvolve optimizes any editable surface (prompt, tools, MCP, skills) of a frozen agent. How: surface as loop parameter, core-owned evaluation (gold isolation, rule P, seal), causal history, two orchestration modes. Why: a fixed surface cannot repair failures that live elsewhere, and self-scored gates promote noise. Crux: pending, so only what/how/why can be judged.

**Abstract (`sections/v2_abstract.tex`).**
- What, how and why are present and in the right order. The why (sentence 1) is the strongest part.
- The crux slot is empty, but the abstract does not even promise a *kind* of finding beyond "headline gain". The two claims the paper must earn are (a) a wider surface helps where failures need enforcement, and (b) the conservative gate buys reliability and not just inertia. Name both as questions answered, so the placeholder has a shape.
- The abstract promises an ablation of "the history" (line 3). The paper says the history ablation was not run (`v2_limitations.tex:11`, `v2_results.tex:48`). `v2_capevolve.tex:40` still says Section 5 "tests whether this history matters by removing it". Fix before submission; this is the most serious consistency problem in the story.
- The title says "Any Editable Surface" and Figure 1 draws four surfaces, but only prompt and tool code are evaluated (`v2_capevolve.tex:9`, `v2_results.tex:53`). Either retitle ("Editable Surfaces") or state in the abstract which two were evaluated. A reviewer will find this in minutes.
- "Code-owned evaluation" is in the title, never defined in the abstract. Use the phrase once in sentence 2 and gloss it.
- The audit of the authors' own earlier results (scorer compliance explains most of the gain) is the most memorable fact in the paper and is absent from the abstract. It is the evidence for the why. One clause would do.
- Abstract sentence 2 is 70 words with a parenthetical and a trailing clause. See sentence S1.

**Introduction (`sections/v2_intro.tex`).**
- Para 1 is background, para 2 gives the two gaps (good, early, crisp), para 3 gives the method, para 4 the evaluation, then contributions. The skeleton is right.
- Too many ideas compete: surface parameter, rule P, two modes, causal context, intake. The guide says to cut anything that does not serve the one finding. Intake (contribution 4) is evidence-light (fault injection only) and splits attention; two modes plus the context are bundled in contribution 3 while the paper has no experiment isolating the context. Candidates to move to the appendix or shrink to one sentence: intake, causal context.
- The method paragraph (para 3) introduces no term the reader can hold: "capability types", "core code", "causal record", "gate". The reader only learns "rule P" from Section 3. The intro never names the central operational idea (two-stage rule P with confirmation re-evaluation) though the abstract calls it "a two-stage promotion rule". Name it here with its operating numbers, in one sentence.
- The "matched rollout checkpoint" is promised (para 4) without saying what it is. One clause: "all arms are compared at the same number of target rollouts".
- The intro does not say what the paper expects the reader to take away on the gate vs surface question. Convey the main messages early: right now it ends with a list of contributions that are mostly nouns ("An agentic intake with a hard adapter gate").
- Contributions list is nominal. Rewrite as verbs (S9).
- Reference to related work is one short sentence. Fine, but the novelty relative to HarnessLens, DarwinX and PACE (which the Limitations bullet admits "overlap") is hidden until the last page. Move one sentence of differentiation up.

**Conclusion (`sections/v2_conclusion.tex`).** Restates the abstract's method sentence almost verbatim. After results are in, replace with two sentences on findings and one on what a practitioner should do.
