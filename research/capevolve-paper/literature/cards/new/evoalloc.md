Editable surface: whatever ShinkaEvolve evolves: Python programs (ADAS-AIME: Python reasoning-harness scaffold around a fixed Qwen3-8B solver; Circle Packing: Python code). EvoAlloc itself only edits the allocation "Strategy" text (not the task artifact).
Search method: underlying search is ShinkaEvolve (island/archive program evolution, LLM proposers GLM-4.5-Air, gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B). EvoAlloc adds an LLM allocator agent (Qwen3-Coder-Next) that, per proposal, picks FULL_EVAL / PARTIAL_EVAL / DISCARD (then CONTINUE_TO_FULL / STOP after partial). Selection/merge/archive unchanged from ShinkaEvolve; no merge.
Memory: yes, in the allocator: "Experience" records (capacity 4 active/tentative + 4 stale, update every 5 new Full outcomes) consolidated into an explicit "Strategy" revised every 10 Full outcomes, validated by shadow comparison on 2 allocation disagreements (Table 1 p13). Memory is of allocation outcomes, not of failed edits.
Feedback signal: Full-eval score (AIME acc averaged over 3 runs x 30 problems; packing sum of radii); Partial = first 8 of 30 AIME problems, single seed.
Task splits / held-out protocol: NONE. "Search and evaluation use the same fixed 30-problem suite" (App B.2, p14); final performance is the best-so-far search score on the same AIME-2024 30 problems. No sealed test. Circle packing is deterministic.
Benchmarks and models: ADAS-AIME (30 AIME 2024 problems, Qwen3-8B solver non-thinking), Circle Packing (n=26). All open-weight, local A100s.
Metrics: best-so-far score vs Full evaluations and vs cumulative LLM tokens; matched-target cost (first index at which 5-seed mean curve hits ShinkaEvolve's final or 95%-gain level).
Budget/cost reporting: Full-eval budget 75 (AIME) and 150 (packing). All Full evals (warm-up, counterfactual, Strategy validation) count toward budget; LLM tokens include proposals, benchmark eval, allocator, self-evolution, shadow validation (App D, p22-23). Allocator tokens 1.02M (AIME) and 1.67M (packing). At full budget EvoAlloc uses MORE total tokens than ShinkaEvolve (p23). Strategy init cost (50 baseline cases from 5 ShinkaEvolve runs) EXCLUDED from reported costs (p13).
Reported limitations: two benchmarks only, single run evaluation layer only, generation/evaluator/update fixed (p9).
Key quantitative results: Fig 3 p7 + Table 5 p23: to reach ShinkaEvolve final target EvoAlloc needs 28 vs 69 Full evals (-59%) and 35.3M vs 89.8M tokens (-61%) on ADAS-AIME; 26 vs 148 (-82%) and 0.7M vs 6.1M tokens (-89%) on Circle Packing. Table 4 p22 (5 seeds, mean +/- pop. sd): AIME final 39.56+/-6.80 vs ShinkaEvolve 35.33+/-2.57 (+12.0%), RPM 36.22+/-1.13, Lineage-UCB 34.00+/-0.89, One-Step 34.89+/-1.94; packing 2.547+/-0.030 vs 2.343+/-0.254 (+8.7%). AIME sd 6.80 means final-score gain is within noise of one sd; matched-target metric is computed on seed-averaged curves.
Candidate-evaluation allocation: LLM-agent allocation among full/partial/discard with evolving textual strategy and 30% counterfactual exploration (Full-evaluate a rejected candidate); baselines Lineage-UCB (Beta posterior, UCB over lineage) and RPM (pairwise LLM tournament). NOT noise-adaptive/racing statistically; no GEPA or MIPRO comparison.
Task selection/curriculum: none (candidate-level gating only; partial = fixed first 8 problems).
Merge/composition (verified?): none.
Optimizer self-modification: the allocation Strategy self-evolves; proposer/evaluator fixed.
Overlap with CapEvolve: claim (a) partially (budget-aware allocation of evaluation across candidates by an LLM agent, cheap-subset triage then full confirmation, token-matched accounting); (e) partially (experience memory, but about allocation decisions); (f) the controller is an LLM agent but for allocation only.
What it means for our claims: (a) cannot be claimed as novel in general; our claim must be narrowed to per-task Bayesian posterior across a candidate DAG plus verified promotion with sealed test. Required comparison/discussion: cheap-subset-then-full triage ablation, and counterfactual exploration of rejected candidates (we should report rejection false-negative rate). Reproduction feasibility: LOW-MED (code unreleased; ShinkaEvolve is public; ideas are re-implementable).
Notes on evidence quality: 5 seeds (good), baselines re-implemented in same framework, but no held-out split (search and report on same 30 AIME problems, i.e., selection bias is part of reported score), wide sd on AIME, small benchmark set, init cost excluded, Qwen3.6/Qwen3-Coder-Next model names unverified.
