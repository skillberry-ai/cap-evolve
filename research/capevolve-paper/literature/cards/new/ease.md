# EASE (slug: ease)
- Title: EASE: Behavior-Adaptive Skill Curation for Self-Evolving Agents; authors Zhen Xiong, Qiaoyu Tan (2; NYU, NYU Shanghai); arXiv 2609.36746v1; first 2026-09-29; preprint (header "Preprint"; no comment/journal_ref).
- Code: abstract states https://github.com/Eric2i/EASE ; GitHub API and web returned 404 on 2026-10-11, so the URL is stated by the authors but not verifiable (repo missing/private/not yet public); license unverified.
- Surface: an online skill repository (add / modify / remove skills) curated by a trained curator LLM; executor frozen.
- Method: not a search over candidates. A single curator policy is trained with GRPO across several frozen executors (Qwen3-8B/32B, GPT-OSS-120B) with retrieval-aware and behavior-aware temporal attribution; at deployment it is conditioned on an online behavioral profile of the executor (recent-window statistics, no extra model calls), current trajectory and retrieved skills, and edits the repository with no further parameter updates (Fig 2 p4). No gate on edits at deployment; edits evaluated by paired edit-rollback diagnostics in analysis only.
- Eval/held-out: ALFWorld (category-macro SR), ScienceWorld, WebShop; three seen executors and three unseen executors (Kimi K2.6, DeepSeek V4 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash) (Table 1 p8, text p7); task orderings varied (six orderings, SR varies <=3.3 pp, App K); no seeds statement found; baselines SkillOS curators and others re-trained/re-run; SkillOS-best is oracle-selected per executor/benchmark (p7).
- Headline: single EASE curator beats matched specialist by 2.7/2.0/1.9 pp avg on seen executors, and SkillOS-best by 3.8/2.7/2.1 pp on unseen executors (p7); repository 34.5-41.0% fewer skills, deployment inference tokens -9.1 to -14.5% (abstract p1); ALFWorld Pareto shift +3.4 to +5.0 pp with 4.5-17.1% fewer tokens (p8, Fig 3).
- Cost: reports deployment-time inference tokens per task, curator descriptor overhead (150-250 tokens); RL training cost/GPU budget not found in text (UNVERIFIED).
- RELEVANCE: low. Learned (RL-trained) curator for retrieved skill libraries, a different regime from test-time search with gated candidates. Only conceptual overlap: executor-dependence of curation (a skill optimized for one executor degrades by 2.3-10.0 pp on another, Table 1 upper p7), which matters for our (d) held-out reporting when changing target model.
