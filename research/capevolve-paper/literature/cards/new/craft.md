Citation count: unavailable
Editable surface: a single prompt (parsed into a tree of sections/rules/examples by SCULPT); objectives are accuracy and prompt-token cost. Prompt-only.
Search method: population of k=4 prompts, R=8 rounds. Refiner (SCULPT critic-actor; OPRO/EvoPrompt swappable) up to nR=8 accuracy variants and condenser (DISTILL) nC=4 compressed variants per parent; Pareto-gap UCB acquisition picks B=2k=8 candidates to validate per round; NSGA-II non-dominated sorting + maximin spread keeps k prompts (Sec 3.2-3.5 p3-5). Bi-objective Pareto front result; no merge, no memory of failed edits.
Feedback signal: task score on validation subset + token count.
Task splits / held-out protocol: D_val partitioned once into T k-means clusters; round t scores on subset V_pi(t) cycling (p4). Held-out test reserved for reporting only (p3). Sizes (Table 7 App B.1): BeaverTails 200 val/500 test; GoEmotions 200/500; DQA 50/175; Causal Judgement 37/130; Formal Fallacies 50/175; Salient Translation 50/175. Test not used for selection as described.
Benchmarks and models: BT, GoE, four BIG-Bench Hard tasks; GPT-5 as optimizer and target (cross-LLM: target GPT-5.4-mini, optimizer varied).
Metrics: Best score, mean retained score, min feasible tokens, peak efficiency, hypervolume, IGD vs method-union front, front size; composite mean rank. Feasible = test score >= 0.95 x initial.
Budget/cost reporting: validation budget per round B x |V_subset| target-LLM calls, fixed across methods ("target-validation compute comparable", p5); optimizer-LLM calls not counted; no dollar/token-of-search table seen.
Reported limitations: English classification/short-answer only; OpenAI targets only; one default refiner/condenser; assumes minimally task-relevant initial prompt (p9).
Key quantitative results: Figure 4 p6 composite mean rank (lower better): CRAFT 2.9 (1st), DISTILL 3.6, WPRO0.5 3.9, WPRO0.7 4.1, WPRO0.3 4.2, SCULPT 4.5, LLMLingua 4.8 (row-to-label mapping inferred from figure text order; text says WPRO0.5 is 3rd and CRAFT is 3rd on cost). Table 1 p7 ablation (single seed): BT CRAFT 63.0 best/512 tokens vs CRAFT-R 63.0/1,013 tokens vs CRAFT-C 53.0/279 tokens; GoE CRAFT 37.0 vs CRAFT-R 41.0/2,130 tokens. Main comparison averages TWO seeds; ablations single seed (p5).
Candidate-evaluation allocation: UCB-based allocation of the per-round validation budget over candidates (mean + beta_t * sigma, beta from ~1 to 0.5), ranked by Pareto-gap distance to the optimistic front; new candidates get default optimistic score beta_t; batch-relative pruning of consistently weak candidates (A.5). It is a bandit/UCB posterior-style allocation across candidates with partial validation and accumulating per-example observations. No successive halving, no noise-adaptive sizing, no full-val confirmation step of the final front (final front uses accumulated partial estimates).
Task selection/curriculum: round-robin over k-means-clustered validation subsets (coverage-style, not adaptive).
Merge/composition (verified?): none (condenser edits "update, delete, or merge nodes" within a prompt tree; no branch merging).
Optimizer self-modification: none.
Overlap with CapEvolve: (a) UCB/optimistic allocation of evaluation among candidates with partial validation and running mean/sd per candidate -- a published predecessor for "posterior/UCB-based allocation across candidates"; cost-aware objective (token cost) also resembles our spend-awareness in a different sense (deployment cost not search spend).
What it means for our claims: (a) cannot be claimed as new if worded as "UCB/optimistic allocation of evaluation across candidates". Our per-task Bayesian posterior over a DAG with full-val confirmation and agent control must be distinguished. Required comparison: UCB-gap acquisition vs our posterior triage at equal call budget (partial feasibility); also consider adding prompt-token cost as a second objective. Reproduction feasibility: MED (code public, GPT-5 dependent).
Notes on evidence quality: 2 seeds for main comparison, single seed ablations, small test sets (130-175 on BBH), no GEPA/MIPRO baseline (baselines are SCULPT, DISTILL, LLMLingua, WPRO variants inside the same loop), search-cost accounting excludes optimizer calls, composite rank aggregates seven correlated metrics (rank-based claims not tested for significance).
