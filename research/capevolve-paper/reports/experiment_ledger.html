Gates and statistical rules

A gate decides whether a candidate replaces the incumbent. It is where noise either gets filtered or gets accepted as progress.

Stock gate (v1 default)
test
paired mean gain > k_se × SE on the selection split
k_se
1.0
footprint
mask tasks the edit cannot affect
coverage
candidate ≥ 0.6 of tasks scored
false accepts
0.87 per 10 null candidates (E22 null pool)
Rule P (registered, frozen)
screen
paired, k_se 1, no footprint
confirm
3 fresh trials, z ≥ 1.282
coverage
joint ≥ 0.95 (veto below)
dmin
0
false accepts
0.23 per 10 null candidates
cost
confirmation ≈ 6× a screen
Rule P2 (v2, amendment 11)
pool
train ∪ val, split once into feedback F and hidden confirm C
round
K candidates in parallel (K=8, or 6 if budget ≤ 600)
rungs
kill-only screens on 12 then 36 F rollouts
confirm
≤ 2 survivors on C against a concurrently re-run incumbent (4 trials)
z_conf
1.7 (round FWER ≈ 0.06–0.08)
K=1
group-sequential test, c = 1.6
coverage
retry missing, quarantine, floor 0.75 + tipping check
